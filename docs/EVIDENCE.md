# Evidence behind Granbot

Granbot is a daily-life companion. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or claim to prevent falls or dementia for a specific person.

## Speech

Polite です／ます, short sentences, the same words in large type, and a slow Japanese voice. The cat’s mouth moves while those words are spoken.

## Movement

Standing routine under age 75 without a fall-risk flag. Chair routine at 75 or older, or when the family sets fall risk. Each day has five short steps, and the set changes across the week. The types stay inside the same evidence: marching in place, heel and toe raises, supported balance, sit-to-stand or a partial stand, and easy arm or shoulder movements. Radio-calisthenics days use only the small supported parts, not jumps. Pain, dizziness, or chest pain stops the session.

Sources: WHO 2020 guidelines on physical activity for older adults (multicomponent balance and strength on 3 or more days); Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Physical Activity Guide 2023, which names radio calisthenics; Cochrane review of exercise for preventing falls in community-dwelling older people (Sherrington et al.), which finds the useful ingredients are balance challenge and lower-limb strength.

## Thinking tasks

Each day has five short prompts, rotating through the week: orientation (date, weekday, season, time of day, or place), a three-word list, reminiscence, naming things in a category, and either recalling those words or remembering one small action. The app does not present this as a dementia test or as commercial brain training.

Sources: cognitive stimulation approaches summarized by Spector and colleagues, and reminiscence work reviewed by Woods and colleagues. These support orientation, word practice, and talking about the past. They do not support a claim that this app prevents dementia.

## Earthquake, rain, and heat

When the Japan Meteorological Agency reports intensity 4 or higher in the household’s prefecture, the iPad turns red, sounds an alarm, and tells the person to get under a sturdy table, protect the head, and stay inside. After the shaking, it names the emergency-kit place and the designated shelter stored by the family. That feed arrives after the quake; it is not an earthquake early warning. Heavy rain, flood, and landslide warnings are spoken from the weather screen and name the same shelter, which is the early-evacuation case for older adults (alert level 3, 高齢者等避難). Heat and cold advice is clothing and water.

Sources: JMA pages on what to do when an earthquake early warning is heard; JMA earthquake list; Cabinet Office evacuation guidelines for 指定緊急避難場所 and 警戒レベル3.
